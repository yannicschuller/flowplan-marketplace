## What the marketplace stores

- **Visitors** (no account): no cookies, no trackers, no analytics services. Only how often a template was downloaded or opened in Flowplan is counted – without IP addresses. Rate limits (e.g. for reports) use the IP address only as a hash that changes every day. If you pick a language in the footer, a cookie remembers that choice.
- **Authors** (with an account): name, e-mail address or GitHub handle, submitted templates and their review status, a session cookie (`mp_session`, httpOnly).
- **Your Flowplan instance** is never known to the marketplace: its address stays in your browser (localStorage) and templates are handed over inside the browser.
